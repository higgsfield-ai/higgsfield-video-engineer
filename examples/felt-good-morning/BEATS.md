# GOOD MORNING! — felt-and-paper kids short (20 s, 1440×1080 4:3, 24 fps)

World: a handmade diorama of felt and paper. Felt has fuzz and visible stitches; hills are layered corrugated cardboard
with cut edges; the moon and sun are painted paper cut-outs hanging on wool yarn; stars are paper on threads;
flowers are pop-up paper; trees are felt cones with button fruit. Miniature: macro lenses, shallow depth of field.

**Logline.** A tiny felt chick crows at dawn, but the paper sun will not rise. It climbs the cardboard hill and finds
the sun asleep on the far end of the moon's yarn, hung over a wooden spool. Hanging on the yarn with all its weight, the
chick hauls the sun up. Twist: the moment it lets go to celebrate, the sun drops and night falls again. So it ties
the yarn to the fat sleeping sheep, who now holds the day up while snoring.

Heroes: **Chik** (yellow felt chick, 6 cm, button eyes, orange felt beak and feet, stitched seam round the belly, two felt
wing flaps) and **Wool** (cream felt sheep, 9 cm, loopy yarn fleece, dark felt face and legs, sleepy).
Props: paper crescent moon, paper sun (round face with stitched closed eyes, cut paper rays), red wool yarn, wooden spool
on a stick gallows at the top of the frame, felt nest, pop-up paper flowers, felt trees with buttons, paper stars.
