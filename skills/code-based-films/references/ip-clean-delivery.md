# IP-clean delivery

Everything in the film must be ours or licensed for the publisher's use.

## Music

- Use our own composed or generated music. Keep the generation record (prompt, model, date, licence terms) with the
  project.
- Library tracks only if the licence explicitly covers the publisher, the platforms and the use (paid or organic).
  Keep the licence document in `$PROJECT/music/`. When in doubt, generate.
- Never use a recognisable melody, theme or sound-alike of a known piece.

## Fonts

- Use fonts under the SIL Open Font License (OFL) or a licence that covers the publisher.
- Keep the font file and its licence text together in `$PROJECT/fonts/` (for example `Font.ttf` plus `OFL.txt`).
- Title rendering is our own code (the example draws cut-paper letters from the font outline).

## Characters and designs

- Characters, props, sets, logos and symbols are our own designs.
- No characters, costumes, logos, symbols, typefaces or signature designs from other studios, games, films, shows
  or brands. No recognisable look-alikes.
- "In the spirit of" means manner only: lighting approach, lens language, pacing, material feel (for example a
  "handmade-craft look" or "dark cinematic anthology look"). Never borrow characters, worlds, iconography or titles.
- Generic animals and everyday objects are fine (a chick, a sheep, a robot vacuum, a paper moon) as long as the
  specific design is ours.
- Text in the frame: our own words, no trademarks, no real product names.

## Reference images

- Generated styleframes and turnarounds are targets only. They never appear in the frame and are never used as
  textures, projections or training material for the film's pixels.
- Prompts for those images must not name protected characters or titles either.

## Final IP checklist (run before delivery)

1. Music: own or generated, with a record; or a licence that covers the publisher, filed with the project.
2. Fonts: OFL or covered licence, licence file present next to the font.
3. Every character, prop and logo in the film is our own design; nothing resembles a known character or brand.
4. No other studio's titles, symbols or iconography in frame, in file names, in titles or in the description.
5. Nothing from `refs/` in any frame (spot-check one frame per shot against the refs).
6. All textures generated by our code; no downloaded assets.
7. SFX synthesised by our code or from a licensed library whose terms cover the publisher.
8. No credentials, internal host names, ids or personal data in the project files, logs or metadata that ship.
9. File metadata clean (title, no stray comments).
10. Delivery folder holds the final film and the requested extras only, clearly named.
